Support

Questions, problems or feedback about Breeathy? Write to us.

Email: dev3@reispace.com

What to include

  • What happened, and what you expected to happen.
  • The steps that lead to the problem, if you can repeat it.
  • Your phone model and its iOS or Android version.
  • The app version, shown at the bottom of Settings in the app.
  • A screenshot, if it helps (please hide any personal information).

Never send your password or the 6-digit codes we email you. We will never ask for them.

If you write about your account, send the email from the address you use for Breeathy so we can find it.
